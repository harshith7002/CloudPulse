PRIORITY 1 Idle Cleanup

Scale down 4 idle instances in us-east-1

batch-job, cache-node, dev-instance, idle-worker all show CPU below 5% for 6+ hours. These are zombie workloads draining cost and carbon for zero business value.

−18.4 kg CO₂/day · Save ₹620/day
aws ec2 stop-instances --instance-ids i-0a1b2c i-0d3e4f i-0g5h6i i-0j7k8l
PRIORITY 2 Scheduling

Enable auto-scaling sleep schedule 11PM–6AM

Night traffic drops 80% but instances run at full capacity. Auto-scaling to 2 nodes during off-peak hours saves significant energy without affecting user experience.

−22.1 kg CO₂/day · Save ₹3,800/mo
aws autoscaling put-scheduled-update-group-action --auto-scaling-group-name prod-asg --recurrence "0 23 * * *" --desired-capacity 2
PRIORITY 3 Migration

Migrate batch jobs → eu-north-1 (Iceland)

Iceland runs on 100% hydro/geothermal power. Carbon intensity 0.012 vs 0.415 kg CO₂/kWh in Virginia. Moving batch workloads cuts their emissions by 97% at zero extra cost.

−31.2 kg CO₂/day · Neutral cost
aws ec2 copy-image --source-region us-east-1 --source-image-id ami-XXXXXXXX --region eu-north-1
PRIORITY 4 Storage

Enable S3 Intelligent-Tiering on all buckets

Cold data sitting in S3 Standard consumes more energy than necessary. Intelligent-Tiering auto-moves unused objects to lower-power archive tiers after 30 days inactivity.

−4.1 kg CO₂/day · Save ₹1,200/mo
aws s3api put-bucket-intelligent-tiering-configuration --bucket MY_BUCKET --id cp --intelligent-tiering-configuration Status=Enabled
